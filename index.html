About Zaura Health

Zaura Health is an advanced AI-powered drug interaction analysis platform developed by Z Corp.® using cutting-edge deep learning technology. Our system analyzes over 20 million drug interaction records to provide comprehensive safety assessments.

Key Features:

  • Advanced neural network with drug embeddings
  • Support for up to 10 drugs per analysis
  • Parallel processing of all drug combinations
  • Real-time confidence scoring
  • CUDA-accelerated predictions

Model Performance:

  • Test Accuracy: 87.52%
  • Model Parameters: 910,274
  • Training Dataset: 20M+ records
  • Drug Vocabulary: 1000+ unique drugs

How to Use

Step 1: Enter Drug Names

Add at least 2 drugs you want to analyze. Use the "Add Another Drug" button to include additional medications.

Step 2: Optional Dosage Information

If available, enter the total number of doses taken in 24 hours. This helps improve prediction accuracy.

Step 3: Analyze

Click "Analyze Drug Interactions" to get comprehensive safety assessment including:

  • Overall safety classification
  • Safety score (0-1)
  • Individual combination results
  • Confidence scores for each prediction

Understanding Results:

  • SAFE: No dangerous interactions detected
  • UNSAFE: Potential dangerous interactions found
  • Confidence: Model's certainty (0-1, higher is more confident)
